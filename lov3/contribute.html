Develop your LOV3 Connection

A connection is demonstrated, not declared. Work through the questions below, then explain your reasoning in the proposal. Similarity alone does not establish causation, shared origin, or practical feasibility.

  1. What are you connecting? Name the specific subjects or observations.
  2. Why are they connected? Describe the mechanism, comparison, or evidence, and consider another explanation.
  3. What does the connection establish? State a bounded conclusion and what remains uncertain.

The template is optional. It stays on this device; completing it does not submit or validate a discovery.
